# Support

LMS Developer provides optional support and consulting for installation,
configuration, security, integrations and production operation.

Qualifying Community use is free. Professional licensing is required for internal
business use at or above US$1 million consolidated annual gross revenue and for
paid customer administration. Support is separate from the software licence.

See [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md) for eligibility and Professional pricing, or
[contact Linux Made Sane](https://www.linuxmadesane.com/contact?topic=support).
