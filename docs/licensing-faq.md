# LMS licensing questions

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Is LMS free? | Yes for personal and homelab use, education, qualifying non-profit use and internal business use below US$1 million annual group revenue. |
| When do I need a paid licence? | Your business needs Professional at US$1 million or more in consolidated annual gross revenue. Paid customer administration and managed services need Professional regardless of revenue. |
| Is that revenue or profit? | Gross revenue, not profit, across your controlling group. Use a documented annual-average exchange rate when converting to USD. |
| Which edition is for professional use? | Professional and Enterprise deployments use LMS Pro. CE is available for a 30-day evaluation of those uses, while qualifying free Community users can continue using CE. Organisation details and usage confirmation are handled on the website. |
| Can I try it first? | Yes. One 30-day evaluation covers your organisation and all its servers. No card is required, and it does not automatically become a paid subscription. |
| Can I read and change the code? | Yes. You can read, build and modify it under the LMS licence. |
| Does the licence change after four years? | No. It stays the LMS licence. There is no automatic conversion to another licence. |
| Can I resell LMS or include it in my own commercial product? | Only with an explicit written commercial agreement. Source access and a standard Professional licence do not grant unrestricted resale, commercial redistribution or rebranding. |
| How much is Professional? | Annual rates are £49 for each of the first 4 servers, £39 for each of the next 15, £29 for each of the next 30, £24 for each of the next 50 and £19 for each of the next 150. At 250 servers or more, contact us for a deployment quote. |
| How do the price bands work? | Each band keeps its own rate. Ten servers cost 4 × £49 plus 6 × £39: £430 per year, about £35.83 per month. |
| How will I pay? | Monthly billing will use the annual rates divided by 12. Online payments are not available yet; contact us to arrange a licence. Any applicable tax and payment terms will be confirmed before payment. |
| Are there user or container charges? | No. Professional is priced per managed physical or virtual server. Community has no arbitrary server, user or container limits. |
| Do I need the hosted Portal? | No. It is an optional, separately priced service. You can use and license LMS without it. |
| Does LMS work offline? | Yes. No mandatory cloud activation, permanent online connection or hidden licensing telemetry is required. |
| Who licenses LMS? | LMS Developer personally. |
| What about a consultant installing it for me? | A one-off installation within your internal IT function follows your organisation's eligibility. Ongoing paid managed services require Professional. |
