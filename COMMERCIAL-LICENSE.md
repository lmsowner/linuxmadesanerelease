# Commercial licensing

LMS Developer is the present personal licensor. Commercial prices and contract terms are agreed separately; no online checkout
is enabled by adoption of the licence. The LMS licence explains which uses require Professional and which distribution
rights need a separate written agreement.

Under the LMS licence, qualifying internal business below
US$1 million consolidated gross revenue is Community; equality/above and
commercial third-party operational use need Professional. No mandatory Enterprise
tier follows from company size. Professional operational permission does not
grant commercial redistribution/OEM/white-label rights without an explicit
rights schedule. Ordinary delegated internal IT and one-off installation solely within a customer's
internal IT function follow the customer's eligibility; a declaration is not a purchase.

One original organisation-wide 30-day evaluation, no card/Portal/account needed.
Signed offline files can grant operational rights; expired terms still require
renewal even while production management remains available for safety.
Professional purchasing is separate from the optional proprietary hosted Portal.
Professional annual rates per server are £49 for 1–4, £39 for 5–19, £29 for
20–49, £24 for 50–99 and £19 for 100–249. Each band keeps its rate; 250+ needs
a deployment quote. Monthly billing will use annual rates divided by 12. Any
applicable taxes and payment terms will be confirmed before payment. Online
payments are not available yet. Portal pricing is separate and by enquiry.
