# LMS trademarks — draft policy

Linux Made Sane and LMS are product marks associated with LMS Developer, not a separate fictional company. This is not a claim of registered trademark status. The licence proposal does not grant broader trademark rights or transfer ownership.

Truthful attribution, compatibility statements and identification of the original project are permitted without implying endorsement. Clearly identify modifications and use distinct branding for public forks where needed to avoid confusion, while retaining copyright/licence/third-party notices. Commercial white-labelling, resale and use implying official partnership require separate permission. See LICENSE, LICENSING.md and COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md; historical terms remain intact.
