# Contributions — licensing transition under review

Do not submit secrets, customer data, private source or material you lack permission to provide. Contributions do not automatically transfer copyright to LMS Developer or to anyone else.

No new CLA, copyright assignment or retroactive grant is enacted in this PR. The [pre-cutover policy](docs/licenses/CONTRIBUTING-pre-cutover.md) is preserved verbatim for evidence of grants previously made; this proposal does not rescind them or establish that every contributor accepted them. New contribution permission under LMS-CSL is **awaiting owner and legal approval**. Do not accept code requiring unresolved relicensing permissions as cleared for the cutover.

Record author identities, origin, employment/commissioned-work rights and upstream notices. Obtain required permissions or replace code before relicensing. Keep THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md and actual upstream notices accurate. Do not label imported work as solely Richard's merely because LMS is maintained by him.

[Ownership assessment and policy recommendation](docs/licensing-ownership.md) explains the existing contributor grant, consent gaps, DCO/CLA options and legal questions. Official DCO 1.1 is not a copyright assignment and uses open-source wording; its suitability must be examined before adopting it for LMS-CSL. A counsel-reviewed, narrowly scoped CLA may be appropriate for dual licensing, but is not silently introduced here.
