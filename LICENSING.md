## Community and Professional

Community is free for personal/homelab, educational institutions' own use,
recognised non-profits' own non-commercial mission/internal use, and internal
business use **below US$1 million consolidated annual gross revenue**. Revenue
means gross revenue, not profit, across the ultimate controlling group and its
controlled entities, eliminating intercompany revenue. Exactly US$1 million
requires Professional. Use a documented published annual-average USD exchange
rate; new businesses use a reasonable documented annualised estimate.

There are no arbitrary Community server/user/container limits or core management
restrictions. Commercial third-party administration and MSP services require
Professional regardless of revenue. Ordinary delegated internal IT and a one-off
installation solely within a customer's internal IT function follow the customer's
eligibility. Ongoing paid managed services are commercial third-party use.
Professional operational permission is distinct from explicit commercial
redistribution/OEM/white-label rights. Company size does not mandate Enterprise.

## Evaluation, agreement and offline operation

One organisation/group-wide **30-day evaluation**, original UTC start inclusive
and end exclusive, needs no card, Portal or online account. Community installation
and licence acceptance do not start it. Adding servers or reinstalling cannot
reset it. Shared/offline records retain the original date. No automatic extension
or trial-to-paid purchase occurs. Declare annually or on change; corrections
never authorise payment.

The About page has separate About, Licensing, Packages & acknowledgements, and Licences & notices tabs. Licence documents use individual tabs rather than expanding sections. User-type selection opens in the LMS-branded
welcome modal; first-use prompts can be closed without recording a choice. Preview licence types and Reset preview let an
administrator inspect each workflow without changing saved declarations, agreements,
licences or evaluation dates; refresh or Exit preview restores the saved view. LMS asks for a user type: home/homelab/hobbyist, education, not-for-profit,
internal business, or commercial customer services. Selecting a type saves the
classification locally; it does not accept terms, start a trial or authorise payment.
Free qualifying Community users do not receive recurring commercial agreement prompts.
Commercial organisations complete their confirmation on the website, including
organisation, use, revenue band and explicit agreement. This is sent to the existing
contact mailbox for review, not treated as paid licence activation or synchronised
back to local entitlements. The local interface does not collect server identities, names, allocations or organisation transfer files. Organisation details and usage confirmation are handled on the website; existing licensing records remain intact.
The full bundled terms can always be read from About. Any explicit local
acceptance record remains tied to the account and exact terms; trusted LAN access
shares an installation-local record.

Expired trials/entitlements, invalid imports, excess capacity and payment outages
leave management operational for safety. Continued operation does not grant
permission for unlicensed use. Signed entitlements verify with allowlisted public
keys, without mandatory heartbeat, hidden telemetry or cloud activation.

Use **Licence prices** or **Discuss support** in Licensing/About,
or [contact Linux Made Sane](https://www.linuxmadesane.com/contact?topic=licensing).

20–49, £24 for 50–99 and £19 for 100–249. Each band keeps its rate; 250+ needs a
deployment quote. Monthly billing will use annual rates divided by 12. Any
applicable tax and payment terms will be confirmed before payment. Online payments
are not yet available.
Professional server licensing is separate from an optional hosted Portal
subscription. No Portal purchase is required or preselected. Portal infrastructure
and billing are proprietary and excluded from the public software grant; no live
hosted service is promised by this licence.
